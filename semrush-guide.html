Semrush: what it does and who it's for

Semrush is an online marketing platform best known for search engine optimization (SEO), meaning the work of getting a website to show up higher in Google results. It also covers paid search research, content planning and competitor tracking.

What Semrush is used for

Keyword research

Find the words people type into search engines, roughly how often they search for them, and how hard it would be to rank for each one. This tells you what to write about and which terms are realistic targets.

Competitor analysis

Enter a competitor's domain and see which keywords bring them traffic, which pages perform best, and which sites link to them. Useful for spotting gaps you can fill.

Site audit

Crawls your website and lists technical problems such as broken links, slow pages or missing page titles, sorted by how serious they are.

Rank tracking

Follows where your pages appear in search results for the keywords you choose, so you can see whether your changes are working.

Who it fits

  • Marketers and agencies who manage search traffic for one or more websites
  • Small business owners who rely on Google to bring in customers
  • Bloggers and content sites that plan articles around search demand

Who may not need it

  • Businesses that get customers mainly from referrals or walk-ins, not search
  • Anyone who only needs a basic keyword idea now and then, where free tools may be enough

Before you pay

  • Check the current plans and limits on the official Semrush site, since they change
  • Use the free trial on a real project, not a test domain
  • Decide which 2–3 reports you'll actually use every week
