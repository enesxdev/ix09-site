Deputy: shift scheduling and timesheets

Deputy is workforce management software for businesses that run on shifts, such as cafés, retail stores, clinics and warehouses. It handles who works when, tracks hours worked, and keeps staff updated.

What it covers

Scheduling

Build weekly rosters, copy them forward, and let staff see their shifts on their phones instead of on a printed sheet.

Time and attendance

Staff clock in and out from a device, and those hours become timesheets that can be checked before payroll.

Shift swaps and leave

Employees can request swaps or time off in the app, and managers approve them in one place.

Who it fits

  • Businesses with hourly staff and changing weekly schedules
  • Managers who spend hours each week building rosters by hand

Who may not need it

  • Small office teams on fixed 9–5 hours
  • Very small businesses with only one or two staff

Before you pay

  • Check that it connects to the payroll system you already use
  • Run one real week of rosters during the trial
  • Get feedback from staff, since they'll use the app every day
