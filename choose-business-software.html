How to pick business software without wasting money

Last updated: November 2026 · Category: How-to

Most bad software purchases have the same cause: the tool was chosen before the problem was clear. This checklist takes about 30 minutes and saves weeks of switching later.

  1. Write the problem in one sentence. For example: "We lose track of which client tasks are overdue."
  2. List who will use it. One person or ten changes the price and the kind of tool you need.
  3. Pick three must-have features. Ignore everything else on the pricing page until these are covered.
  4. Check what it must connect to. Email, calendar, payroll, your website. A tool that doesn't connect creates double work.
  5. Shortlist two or three options. More than that and you won't test any of them properly.
  6. Run the trial on real work. Use a live project, not sample data, and set a reminder before the trial ends.
  7. Price it at your size next year. Seats, contacts and usage limits often grow faster than expected.
