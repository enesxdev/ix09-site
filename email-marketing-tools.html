How to choose an email marketing tool

Last updated: November 2026 · Category: Email marketing

An email marketing tool lets you collect subscribers, send newsletters and set up automatic emails, such as a welcome message when someone signs up. Most look similar at first, so it helps to know what actually separates them.

The features that matter

Automation

Sequences that send on their own based on what a subscriber does: signing up, clicking a link, or not opening for a while. This is where most of the time savings come from.

Signup forms and landing pages

Built-in forms and simple pages to collect emails without needing a separate website builder.

Deliverability

How reliably your emails land in the inbox instead of spam. Hard to judge from a features page, so test with your own addresses during a trial.

Pricing model

Most tools charge by number of subscribers, some by emails sent. Check how the price grows as your list grows, not just the starting price.
